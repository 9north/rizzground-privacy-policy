# Privacy Policy for RizzGround

**Effective Date:** September 28, 2026
**App Name:** RizzGround
**Package Name:** `com.rizztix.rizzground`
**Developer / Entity:** Rizztix

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to RizzGround ("we," "our," or "us").

RizzGround is a professional on-ground sales, ticketing, table booking, and event management application designed for event staff, promoters, and authorized personnel within the Rizztix ecosystem.

This Privacy Policy explains how RizzGround collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use our mobile application. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with its terms, please do not access or use the application.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide a secure and efficient on-ground event management, sales, and ticketing experience.

### A. Information You Provide Directly

* **Authentication Information:** Username, employee or staff identifier, and authentication credentials or tokens required to access authorized staff accounts.

* **Transaction and Sales Data:** Information related to ticket sales, table bookings, customer names, contact numbers, ticket types, payment status, and transaction or confirmation details entered during on-ground operations.

* **Customer Information:** Information collected for ticketing, reservations, and table bookings as required by event organizers, venues, or applicable operational requirements.

### B. Information Collected Automatically

* **Device and Network Information:** Information about device and network connectivity necessary for the application to communicate securely with Rizztix servers and process live transactions. The application may use Android permissions such as `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` and `INTERNET` for these purposes.

* **Push Notifications:** If notification permission is granted, the application may use the `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` permission to send real-time alerts related to ticket orders, sales activity, operational updates, and system announcements.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use information collected through RizzGround for the following business and operational purposes:

* **Authentication and Access Control:** To verify staff identity and provide authorized access to event management, ticketing, booking, and sales tools.

* **Ticketing and Sales Operations:** To process ticket sales, manage table bookings, record customer information, maintain transaction records, and generate operational or sales reports.

* **Communication and Support:** To send transaction confirmations, operational updates, event-related information, system announcements, and other notifications relevant to authorized users.

* **Security and Fraud Prevention:** To maintain system security, monitor application activity, maintain audit records, detect unauthorized access, and help prevent fraudulent or suspicious transactions.

* **Business Operations and Reporting:** To generate reports and operational records required for event management, reconciliation, accounting, and internal business processes.

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent personal information to third parties.

We may share information in the following circumstances:

* **Event Organizers and Venues:** Sales records, booking information, attendance information, and related operational data may be shared with authorized event organizers and venues associated with the relevant event.

* **Service Providers:** We may share information with trusted technology, hosting, infrastructure, API, communication, or security service providers that assist us in operating RizzGround and the Rizztix platform.

* **Business Operations:** Information may be shared within Rizztix or with authorized personnel where necessary to manage events, provide support, reconcile transactions, investigate issues, or operate our services.

* **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose information when required by applicable law, regulation, court order, subpoena, or other valid legal process, or when necessary to protect the rights, property, security, or safety of Rizztix, our users, staff, customers, or others.

## 5. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational security measures designed to protect personal information, transaction data, and staff credentials.

These measures may include secure authentication, protected credential or token storage, access controls, and encrypted HTTPS communications between the application and our servers.

However, no method of electronic transmission or data storage is completely secure. Therefore, while we take reasonable steps to protect information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain staff account information, authentication records, sales transactions, booking information, event data, audit records, and related information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

* provide and operate our services;
* fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy;
* comply with legal, accounting, tax, or regulatory requirements;
* resolve disputes;
* prevent fraud and maintain security; and
* enforce our agreements and policies.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and applicable legal or business requirements.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

These may include:

* **Access and Correction:** You may request access to or correction of your account information by contacting your organization administrator or Rizztix support, where applicable.

* **Notification Controls:** You may enable or disable push notifications through your Android device settings.

* **Account Deactivation:** If you no longer require access to RizzGround, you may contact your system or organization administrator to request deactivation of your staff account.

* **Privacy Requests:** Where required by applicable law, you may have additional rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other actions concerning your personal information.

## 8. Children's Privacy

RizzGround is a business and staff-oriented application intended for authorized event personnel and is not designed for use by children.

We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information directly from children under the age of 13 through RizzGround.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child in violation of applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to delete or otherwise handle that information as required.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, legal requirements, security practices, or business operations.

When we make changes, we will update the **Effective Date** at the top of this Privacy Policy. Where required by law, we may also provide additional notice of material changes.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how information is handled.
